WHAT THE APP DOES

myCareer is a structured writing tool for reconstructing a work history in long form,
then exporting it as a Markdown file the person keeps.

It is not a note-taking app with an export button. The structure is the product:

1. PROMPT SETS. Each screen carries the questions that pull specifics out of a vague
   memory: what would have broken if you had not been there, who else was in the room,
   what went wrong, who could contradict this account. These are drawn from a
   documented interview protocol and are the reason the writing produced here differs
   from writing produced in a blank text field.

2. THE AGENCY DISTINCTION. Every entry is recorded as one of three claims: the person
   did it, scoped it and handed it off, or enabled a team to do it. The app does not
   let these collapse into one another, because they are materially different claims
   to make to an employer.

3. PROVENANCE ON NUMBERS. Each figure carries what it measures, its baseline, and
   where its source could be produced from, plus a flag for whether the person can
   actually produce that source today. Unsourced figures are marked in the editor and
   segregated into their own section of the export, headed "Numbers that still need a
   source."

4. THE EXPORT. Renders the whole record as Markdown, including the provenance tables
   and the unsourced-numbers section, and hands it to the share sheet.

TO REVIEW IT

The app opens empty. Please use the sample content rather than reviewing empty text
fields:

  1. Tap "Add a chapter" and fill in a title, organization, and dates.
  2. Write a paragraph in "The chapter". Expand "What to answer here" to see the
     prompt set.
  3. Tap "Add something you did", set the agency picker, and add a number. Toggle
     "I can produce that source today" off to see the flagging behavior.
  4. Tap the export icon in the navigation bar. The rendered Markdown appears with
     an unsourced-numbers section at the bottom; "Save" opens the share sheet.

PRIVACY AND NETWORKING

The app contains no networking code of any kind. There are no SDKs, no analytics, no
accounts, no sign-in, no sync, and no advertising. Data is written to one JSON file in
the app's Documents directory. UIFileSharingEnabled and LSSupportsOpeningDocumentsInPlace
are set so the user can retrieve their own writing through the Files app without this
app installed, which is deliberate: the record is intended to outlive the app.

The privacy manifest declares no tracking, no collected data types, and no
required-reason API usage, all of which are accurate.

No account is required, so no demo credentials are needed.
