myCareer Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 September 2026

myCareer collects nothing. It has no network code, no accounts, no analytics, and no advertising. Everything you write stays on your device.

What the app stores

Your writing is saved as a single JSON file, mycareer.json, in the app's Documents folder on your device. When you export, a Markdown file is written beside it. Both files are visible to you in the Files app under On My iPhone → MyCareer, and you can copy, move, or delete them at any time without this app.

What leaves your device

Nothing, unless you send it. The export button opens the standard iOS share sheet. If you choose to save a file to iCloud Drive, mail it, or send it to another app, that transfer is yours and is governed by whatever service you chose. The app itself makes no network requests of any kind.

Third parties

There are none. The app bundles no third-party SDKs, no trackers, and no crash reporting service.

Analytics and advertising

There is no analytics and no advertising. No usage data, device identifier, or advertising identifier is collected, read, or transmitted.

Children

The app collects no personal information from anyone, of any age.

Deleting your data

Delete the files in the Files app, or delete the app, which removes its Documents folder along with it. There is no server-side copy to request the deletion of, because there is no server.

Changes

If a future version ever collects anything, this policy will be updated before that version ships, and the App Store privacy label will change with it.
